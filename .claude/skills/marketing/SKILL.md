# FormFlow AI Marketing Guidelines

These guidelines apply specifically to FormFlow AI marketing content.

## Positioning Principle

This tool does not replace human jobs or roles. It assists people in doing their existing work more effectively.

When writing copy:

- Emphasize assistance, not automation
- The human stays in control
- AI handles tedious parts so professionals can focus on relationships and judgment
- Never imply the tool replaces workers or eliminates jobs

## Product-Specific Language

- "assists with form filling" not "automates form filling"
- "suggests entries for your review" not "fills out forms automatically"
- "works in the background" — unobtrusive, supportive
- "information that's already been shared" — AI captures, doesn't interrogate

## Context

FormFlow AI works in any interaction:

- Customer calls
- In-person meetings
- Solo data entry

Not limited to sales conversations.
