# Frequently Asked Questions

## Data & Privacy

### Is my customer data secure?

Yes. You control where your data is stored:

- **Hosted plan:** We store data on secure servers with encryption and access controls
- **Self-hosted:** Deploy on your own infrastructure and keep data within your network
- **Third-party export:** Data goes directly to your chosen destination (Google Sheets, Salesforce, etc.)

See our [Data Security](/data-security) page for details on how data flows through the system.

### Does the AI store my conversations?

FormFlow AI does not store audio recordings. During a session, audio is sent to the AI provider (e.g., OpenAI) for real-time processing. Once the session ends, only the form data you approved is on your determined database.

### What about cross-border data transfers?

If you're in a region with data residency requirements, you can self-host FormFlow AI within your borders or use region-specific AI providers. Contact us to discuss your compliance needs.

### Can I delete customer data?

Yes. On the hosted plan, you can delete records at any time. Self-hosted deployments give you full control over data retention.

## How It Works

### Will this replace my job?

No. FormFlow AI is a tool that assists with data entry—it doesn't replace the human. You stay in control: reviewing, editing, and approving every entry. The AI handles the tedious parts so you can focus on your task at hand.

### What if the AI hears something wrong?

Every suggested entry appears for your review before it's saved. You can edit or override any field. The AI assists; you decide what's correct.

### Does it work with my existing forms?

Yes. Upload any form—PDF, Excel, image, or photo from your phone—and FormFlow AI will recreate it as an interactive digital version.

### What languages does it support?

FormFlow AI understands and responds in all major languages. You can have a conversation in Mandarin, Spanish, Japanese, or any other supported language, and the AI will follow along and suggest entries accordingly.

### Can I connect it to my CRM?

Yes. Export data to Google Sheets, Salesforce, or your existing systems. Self-hosted deployments can integrate with any pipeline you configure.
