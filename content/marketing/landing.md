# FormFlow AI

Voice-powered form filling for customer conversations

---

## What is FormFlow AI?

FormFlow AI is your data entry assistant. It listens during customer conversations and fills in form entries in real-time. You can review, edit, or approve each field—while spending less time typing and more time with your task at hand.

---

## How It Works

1. **Upload your form** — Bring any existing form: PDF, Excel, image, or snap a photo from your phone
2. **We recreate it** — FormFlow AI converts your form into an interactive, stylized version
3. **Start your conversation** — Begin talking with your customer as you normally would
4. **AI suggests entries** — As details are mentioned, the AI proposes field values for your review
5. **AI catches gaps** — The agent flags missing or incorrect information and prompts for clarification
6. **Data goes where you need it** — Export to Google Sheets, Salesforce, your CRM, or any existing pipeline

---

## Who Is This For?

FormFlow AI is built for professionals who spend time onboarding customers through information-heavy processes:

- **Financial advisors** onboarding new investors with suitability questionnaires
- **Bank representatives** opening accounts or processing loan applications
- **Mortgage officers** gathering borrower information
- **Insurance agents** completing policy applications
- **Healthcare staff** conducting patient intake
- **Legal professionals** collecting client information

If your job involves talking to customers while filling out forms, FormFlow AI can help.

## Why FormFlow AI?

### Focus on your customer, not your keyboard

Maintain eye contact and build rapport while the AI assists with data entry.

### Keep your existing forms

Already have approved forms that legal or compliance signed off on? No need to change them. Upload your PDF, spreadsheet, or photo—we adapt to your format, not the other way around.

### Fits your existing workflow

Data exports to wherever you need it—the same Excel format you already use, your existing database, your CRM. We integrate with your process.

### AI that supports the conversation

The agent prompts for missing fields and answers customer questions using your knowledge base.

### You stay in control

Every suggested entry can be reviewed, edited, or overridden. The AI assists; you decide.

### Fewer errors

Real-time validation catches incomplete or incorrect entries before submission.

### Faster onboarding

Complete forms in the time it takes to have a conversation.

### Works in any language

Speak in Mandarin, Spanish, Japanese, or any major language. The AI follows along and suggests entries accordingly.
