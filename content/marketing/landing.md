# FormFlow AI

**Voice-powered form filling for customer conversations**

---

## What is FormFlow AI?

FormFlow AI is an intelligent assistant that listens to your customer conversations and automatically fills out forms in real-time. No more typing while talking. No more asking customers to repeat themselves. Just natural conversation while the form completes itself.

---

## How It Works

1. **Start your conversation** — Begin talking with your customer as you normally would
2. **The agent listens** — FormFlow AI picks up relevant information from the conversation
3. **Forms fill automatically** — As details are mentioned, the corresponding fields populate
4. **Review and submit** — Verify the captured information and submit

---

## Who Is This For?

FormFlow AI is built for professionals who spend time onboarding customers through information-heavy processes:

- **Financial advisors** onboarding new investors with suitability questionnaires
- **Bank representatives** opening accounts or processing loan applications
- **Mortgage officers** gathering borrower information
- **Insurance agents** completing policy applications
- **Healthcare staff** conducting patient intake
- **Legal professionals** collecting client information

If your job involves talking to customers while filling out forms, FormFlow AI can help.
