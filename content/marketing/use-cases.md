# Use Cases

Real scenarios where FormFlow AI helps professionals work more effectively.

---

## Financial Services

### Financial Advisors

**The situation:** Onboarding a new investor requires completing suitability questionnaires, risk assessments, and account applications—often 10+ pages of forms.

**The problem:** Advisors split attention between the client conversation and data entry. Important details get missed. The experience feels transactional rather than personal.

**With FormFlow AI:** The advisor focuses entirely on understanding the client's goals. The AI listens and suggests entries for income, risk tolerance, investment timeline, and more. The advisor reviews and approves, completing paperwork in the natural flow of conversation.

---

### Bank Representatives

**The situation:** Opening accounts, processing loans, handling credit applications—each requires capturing detailed customer information.

**The problem:** Customers repeat themselves. Representatives ask the same questions to fill required fields. The process feels slow and bureaucratic.

**With FormFlow AI:** Information mentioned once gets captured once. The AI prompts for missing fields naturally. Representatives can explain products and answer questions without breaking to type.

---

### Mortgage Officers

**The situation:** Gathering borrower information involves employment history, income verification, property details, and more.

**The problem:** Mortgage applications are long. Customers get frustrated answering the same questions across multiple forms.

**With FormFlow AI:** Details flow from conversation to form. The officer can walk through the process conversationally while the AI handles the documentation.

---

## Insurance

### Insurance Agents

**The situation:** Policy applications require personal details, coverage preferences, risk factors, and beneficiary information.

**The problem:** Agents juggle building rapport with getting required information. Rushing through questions makes customers feel like a number.

**With FormFlow AI:** The agent has a real conversation about coverage needs. The AI captures relevant details as they come up naturally, flagging anything missing before submission.

---

## Healthcare

### Patient Intake Staff

**The situation:** New patients need to provide medical history, current medications, insurance information, and consent forms.

**The problem:** Patients are often stressed or unwell. Typing while they talk feels impersonal. Handwritten forms are hard to read and require re-entry.

**With FormFlow AI:** Staff can focus on making patients comfortable. A simple conversation captures the needed information, and staff review before finalizing.

---

## Legal

### Law Firm Intake

**The situation:** Client onboarding requires case details, personal information, conflict checks, and engagement terms.

**The problem:** Attorneys bill for their time. Spending it on data entry isn't the best use of expertise.

**With FormFlow AI:** The initial client conversation captures case facts and contact information. Attorneys focus on understanding the legal situation while the AI handles documentation.

---

## Field Services

### Service Technicians

**The situation:** Completing service reports on-site requires documenting work performed, parts used, and customer sign-off.

**The problem:** Technicians fill out forms on tablets or paper while trying to explain the work. It slows down the job.

**With FormFlow AI:** The technician explains the work to the customer, and the AI captures it for the service report. Review and submit before leaving the site.

---

## Real Estate

### Real Estate Agents

**The situation:** Buyer and seller intake involves understanding preferences, financial readiness, and property requirements.

**The problem:** Agents take notes during showings and calls, then transfer to CRM later. Details get lost.

**With FormFlow AI:** Conversations during showings or calls are captured in real-time. Preferences, feedback, and next steps populate directly into the agent's system.

---

## Government & Public Sector

### Benefits Applications

**The situation:** Citizens applying for benefits need to provide eligibility information, documentation, and personal details.

**The problem:** Applications are complex. Citizens struggle with forms. Staff spend time explaining and re-entering information.

**With FormFlow AI:** Staff walk applicants through the process conversationally. The AI captures responses and flags incomplete sections before submission.
