# Product Context

## What This App Does

An AI-powered voice assistant that listens to conversations and automatically fills out forms in real-time. The agent sits on the side during human-to-human conversations and extracts relevant information to populate form fields as people speak.

## Primary Use Case

Sales professionals and client-facing staff who need to onboard new customers through lengthy information-gathering processes. Instead of manually typing information into forms while talking to customers, the agent handles form-filling automatically.

## Target Industries / Scenarios

- **Financial services**: New investor onboarding at hedge funds, wealth management firms
- **Banking**: Account opening, loan applications, financial suitability questionnaires
- **Mortgage**: Mortgage applications requiring extensive borrower information
- **Insurance**: Policy applications, claims intake
- **Healthcare**: Patient intake forms, medical history questionnaires
- **Legal**: Client intake, case information gathering
- **Any domain** where onboarding requires collecting substantial upfront information through conversation

## How It Works

1. Sales person starts a conversation with their customer (in person or on a call)
2. The agent listens to the conversation via microphone
3. As relevant information is spoken, the agent automatically fills corresponding form fields
4. The sales person can focus on the customer relationship rather than data entry

## Key Value Proposition

- Reduces friction in customer onboarding conversations
- Allows sales staff to maintain eye contact and rapport with customers
- Speeds up form completion
- Reduces data entry errors from manual transcription
